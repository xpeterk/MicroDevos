## 3 · A Refuge That Does Not Move
*Five quiet seconds.*
**Name the trouble, then say the verse once.**

When fear is loud, peace is still a prayer. The ground can shift. The refuge does not.

> God is our refuge and strength, a very present help in trouble. — Psalm 46:1 KJV

Today, say the verse once before the next hard conversation.
